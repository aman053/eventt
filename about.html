ABOUT EVENTEASE

Planning should feel easy.

EventEase is a Progressive Web App designed to bring the moving pieces of event planning into one clear workspace.

01

Simple by design

Traditional HTML, CSS and JavaScript keep the project easy to understand, maintain and explain during a college viva.

02

Useful in practice

Events, guests, tasks, expenses and venue information can be managed together instead of being scattered across notes and spreadsheets.

03

Ready for the web

The manifest and service worker make EventEase installable as a PWA, while the optional Vercel API securely connects the AI assistant.
