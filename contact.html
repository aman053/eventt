FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common EventEase questions.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create Account, enter your name, email and password, and EventEase signs you in immediately.

Can I manage guests and tasks?

Yes. The dashboard includes dedicated areas for guests, tasks, budgets, expenses and venue details.

Can I use EventEase on my phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive and the application is configured as a Progressive Web App.

Does the AI chatbot answer event-planning questions?

Yes. When the Experiential Labs API is configured, EventEase AI can answer planning questions through the chatbot.

How do I send feedback?

Use the feedback form below. It opens your email application with the feedback details prepared for you.
